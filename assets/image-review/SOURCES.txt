The graph takes RGB pixels resized to 224 by 224, channel-first, float32 in
[0,255]. Its outputs are probabilities for gore (NSFL), pornographic/highly
suggestive content (NSFW) and other images (SFW), in that order. The homepage
requires SFW >= 0.90 with NSFL and NSFW each < 0.10. These are site thresholds.
The model card notes fewer NSFL training examples. The model can miss unsafe
material and flag ordinary images. It does not classify non-graphic violence,
hate symbols or text within images.
